IS117 · GROUP BUSINESS · RESEARCH REVIEW 1

Research before we build.

Our question: What do college students need in an everyday backpack, and how can CAMPUS LOOP communicate practical features, affordability, and simple style?

Research status: Competitor pages were reviewed on November 17, 2026. Customer personas below are hypotheses, not interviews. CAMPUS LOOP features are proposed decisions, not tested results.

01 / Competitor research

We reviewed official product pages to see how established brands communicate capacity, laptop protection, organization, materials, and everyday use. The observations below describe what the linked pages say; our interpretations are labeled separately.

Competitor 01Campus-focused

JanSport — Campus Pack

Open the original JanSport product page ↗

Evidence observed: The product page lists two large compartments, two side water-bottle pockets, an external padded 16-inch laptop sleeve, an ergonomic S-curve shoulder strap design, a padded back panel, and a 32-liter capacity. It lists 100% recycled 600-denier polyester for the main body fabric.

Our interpretation: JanSport makes practical campus features easy to identify. This supports our idea of showing storage, laptop fit, and comfort details clearly on a student-focused product page. It does not prove that all students prioritize those features.

Competitor 02Everyday style

Herschel — Classic Backpack XL

Open the original Herschel product page ↗

Evidence observed: The page lists a 30-liter volume, a padded sleeve for a 15- or 16-inch laptop, a water-bottle pocket, and a front pocket. It describes the bag as made with 100% recycled 600D polyester, excluding trims, and lists adjustable padded shoulder straps.

Our interpretation: Herschel combines a simple visual style with specific everyday-use details. This supports a clean product layout that pairs photographs with clear specifications. We should not assume the visual style alone drives customer choice.

Competitor 03Multi-use

Fjällräven — Classic Kånken

Open the original Fjällräven product page ↗

Evidence observed: The page lists a 16-liter volume and a weight of 300 grams. It describes Vinylon F fabric as hard-wearing, water-resistant, and quick-drying. The product has a large main compartment, two flat side pockets, a front zippered pocket, and a removable seat pad. The page describes use for school, work, trekking, and travel.

Our interpretation: Fjällräven presents one backpack for several daily activities and explains practical design details. This supports showing how a product could fit different parts of a student's routine. It does not prove our proposed audience wants a smaller bag.

02 / Two positioning alternatives

Alternative A

Practical campus backpack

Focus on college students who need organized storage for a laptop, books, chargers, and daily essentials. The brand emphasizes function, comfort, accessible pricing, and straightforward product information.

Alternative B

Style-focused lifestyle backpack

Focus on students and young adults who want a backpack for classes, social activities, and weekend use. The brand emphasizes colors, appearance, personal style, and versatility.

Proposed choice: Alternative A. We choose the practical campus direction because the JanSport and Herschel pages provide clear examples of laptop storage and organization features that can be explained to a student audience. This is our research-based design choice, not proof of customer demand.

03 / Two persona hypotheses

The following personas are fictional planning tools based on assumptions. We have not interviewed these people or observed them as customers.

Hypothesis 01

The commuter student

Situation: Travels to campus and may also work part-time.

Possible needs: Laptop storage, room for notebooks and chargers, water-bottle pockets, and comfortable straps.

Assumption to test: Commuter students may value organization and comfort more than decorative details.

Hypothesis 02

The style-conscious student

Situation: Attends classes and spends time with friends or in campus activities.

Possible needs: A simple-looking backpack that matches different outfits and works for everyday use.

Assumption to test: Students may choose a simple style when it also offers useful storage and an accessible price.

Proposed primary audience: The commuter student. We select this audience because the concept emphasizes practical campus use. This choice should be revisited after feedback from real students.

04 / Brand and design direction

  • Brand name: CAMPUS LOOP.
  • Brand promise: Simple, comfortable, practical backpacks that help college students carry everyday essentials without paying premium prices.
  • Brand character: Friendly, practical, approachable, and student-focused.
  • Visual direction: A clean layout, neutral colors, clear product photographs, simple navigation, and readable product specifications.
  • Honest persuasion: Do not use fake reviews, invented discounts, false urgency, or unsupported claims such as “waterproof.” Show verified dimensions, capacity, laptop compatibility, materials, price, and policies.
