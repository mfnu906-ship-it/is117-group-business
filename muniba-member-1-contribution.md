## 1. Research responsibility: JanSport Campus Pack

**Original source:** [JanSport — Campus Pack](https://www.jansport.com/en-us/products/campus-pack)  
**Date checked:** November 17, 2026

### Evidence from the official product page

The JanSport Campus Pack page lists:

- Two large compartments.
- Two side water-bottle pockets.
- An external padded laptop sleeve for a 16-inch laptop.
- Ergonomic S-curve shoulder straps and a fully padded back panel.
- A capacity of 32 liters.
- Main-body fabric described as 100% recycled 600-denier polyester.
- Product dimensions of 19 × 13 × 8 inches and a listed weight of 20.5 ounces (0.58 kg).

These are manufacturer-published product details, not independent test results. The page displayed a price of $62 when checked; price and availability can change and should be rechecked before being used in a price comparison.

### Interpretation for CAMPUS LOOP

JanSport gives customers several practical details that are easy to scan: compartments, bottle pockets, laptop fit, capacity, and dimensions. This suggests CAMPUS LOOP should make the same types of information easy to find rather than rely only on a product photograph or a general statement such as “great for college.”

The source does not prove that every college student wants a 32-liter bag or that the same features should be copied. It only provides an example of how a competitor presents a campus-oriented backpack.

## 2. Research responsibility: Herschel Classic Backpack XL

**Original source:** [Herschel Supply Co. — Classic Backpack XL](https://herschel.com/products/classic-backpack-xl)  
**Date checked:** November 17, 2026

### Evidence from the official product page

The Herschel Classic Backpack XL page lists:

- A 30-liter volume.
- A floating padded sleeve that fits a 15- or 16-inch laptop; the sleeve dimensions are listed as 12 inches high by 11 inches wide.
- A water-bottle pocket and a front pocket.
- Adjustable padded shoulder straps.
- 100% recycled 600D polyester, excluding trims, and a liner made from 100% recycled polyester.
- Product dimensions of 17.72 × 12.8 × 6.5 inches and a listed weight of 1.21 pounds (0.55 kg).
- A limited lifetime warranty, with coverage details subject to the manufacturer's warranty terms.

When checked, the product page displayed $70 and marked the selected product Out of Stock. This is a dated page observation, not a stable price or a claim about all colors or retailers.

### Interpretation for CAMPUS LOOP

Herschel combines a simple everyday design with a feature list and product specifications. This suggests CAMPUS LOOP can use a clean product layout while still showing practical details such as laptop-sleeve dimensions, volume, pockets, materials, and weight.

The competitor page does not establish that Herschel's appearance causes customers to buy the backpack, nor does it tell us what price our target audience would accept. Those questions require customer research.

## 3. Comparison of the two competitors

| Comparison point | JanSport Campus Pack | Herschel Classic Backpack XL | Possible implication for CAMPUS LOOP |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capacity | 32 L | 30 L | Display the actual capacity so customers can judge the size. Do not choose a capacity without checking the target audience's needs. |
| Laptop fit | External padded 16-inch sleeve | Floating padded sleeve fits a 15/16-inch laptop; sleeve dimensions are listed | State laptop compatibility carefully and include sleeve dimensions. |
| Organization | Two large compartments and two side bottle pockets | Front pocket and bottle pocket are listed | Explain where common items can be stored instead of using vague claims. |
| Comfort details | S-curve shoulder straps and padded back panel | Adjustable padded shoulder straps | Present comfort-related design features factually; do not claim a product is comfortable without testing it. |
| Material | 100% recycled 600D polyester main-body fabric listed | 100% recycled 600D polyester, excluding trims; recycled-polyester liner | Identify exact material coverage and exclusions. Do not make broad sustainability claims from one material detail. |
| Page presentation | Product benefits and specifications | Feature bullets, dimensions, weight, volume, shipping/returns/warranty details | Organize product information into scannable sections and make policies clear when they exist. |

### Finding and proposed design decision

**Finding:** Both official product pages make laptop compatibility, storage, and capacity visible as product information. JanSport specifies a 16-inch padded sleeve and 32 L capacity; Herschel specifies a floating 15/16-inch laptop sleeve and 30 L capacity.

**Proposed CAMPUS LOOP decision:** Include a “Product details” area that clearly states verified capacity, laptop compatibility and sleeve dimensions, compartments/pockets, material, dimensions, and weight. Only publish values that are true for the final CAMPUS LOOP product.
