## 1. Fjällräven Kånken Product Research

**Official Source:** https://www.fjallraven.com/us/en-us/bags-gear/kanken/kanken-bags/kanken/

**Date Checked:** November 23, 2026

### Product Features

According to Fjällräven's official product page, the Classic Kånken backpack includes:

- Capacity: 16 liters.
- Weight: 300 grams.
- Material: Vinylon F fabric.
- A large main compartment with a wide zippered opening.
- Two flat side pockets and one front zippered pocket.
- A removable seat pad.
- Adjustable shoulder straps and top handles.
- A reflective logo.
- Dimensions: 36 × 27.5 × 12 cm.

Fjällräven describes the backpack as suitable for school, work, trekking, and travel. The manufacturer also describes the material as water-resistant and quick-drying.

These are manufacturer-published specifications, not independently tested results.

### Research Observation

Fjällräven presents a simple backpack design while clearly explaining its storage, size, material, and everyday uses.

### What This Means for CAMPUS LOOP

This research suggests that CAMPUS LOOP should provide clear product descriptions and specifications so students can understand what a backpack offers before making a decision.

However, the product page does not prove that college students prefer smaller backpacks or that CAMPUS LOOP should use the same features.

## 2. Two Positioning Alternatives

### Alternative A: Practical Campus Backpack

This direction focuses on college students who need backpacks for classes, commuting, and everyday activities.

The brand would emphasize:

- Organized storage for school essentials.
- Laptop compatibility when verified.
- Comfortable everyday use.
- Simple design.
- Affordable pricing as a proposed goal.

### Alternative B: Style-Focused Lifestyle Backpack

This direction focuses on students and young adults who care about appearance and personal style.

The brand would emphasize:

- Modern colors and designs.
- Fashion and personal expression.
- Versatility for school and social activities.
- Simple and attractive product presentation.

### Selected Positioning: Alternative A

Our group selected the practical campus backpack direction because our competitor research highlights useful features such as storage, capacity, laptop compartments, and everyday functionality.

We believe this direction gives CAMPUS LOOP a clearer purpose for college students, especially commuters.

This is our proposed business decision, not proof of customer demand.

## 3. Two Student Persona Hypotheses

The following personas are fictional assumptions created for planning. They are not based on actual interviews.

### Persona 1: The Commuter Student

**Situation:** A college student who travels between home, campus, and possibly a part-time job.

**Possible Needs:**

- Storage for a laptop, notebooks, and chargers.
- Space for everyday essentials.
- Organized compartments.
- Comfortable shoulder straps.
- Affordable pricing.

**Assumption:** Commuter students may prioritize organization and practical features over appearance.

**How to Test:** Ask actual commuter students what they carry and which backpack features they consider most important.

### Persona 2: The Style-Conscious Student

**Situation:** A student who attends classes and participates in campus or social activities.

**Possible Needs:**

- A simple and attractive backpack.
- Different color options.
- Useful storage compartments.
- A design suitable for school and everyday activities.

**Assumption:** Some students may prefer backpacks that combine practical storage with personal style.

**How to Test:** Ask students to compare backpack designs and explain which features influence their preferences.

### Selected Primary Audience

Our proposed primary audience is commuter college students.

We selected this audience because CAMPUS LOOP focuses on practical backpacks for everyday campus routines.

We still need feedback from actual students to determine whether our assumptions are accurate.

## 4. CAMPUS LOOP Brand and Website Decisions

### Brand Direction

CAMPUS LOOP is a proposed backpack brand focused on practicality, simplicity, and student needs.

**Brand Character:** Friendly, practical, approachable, and student-focused.

**Brand Message:** Carry your day. Keep it simple.

### Research-Based Website Decisions

**Decision 1: Clear Product Information**

Fjällräven provides detailed information about capacity, weight, materials, and storage.

CAMPUS LOOP should use a clear product-details section containing verified specifications.

**Decision 2: Practical Product Descriptions**

Fjällräven explains how its backpack can be used for school, work, and travel.

CAMPUS LOOP should explain how its proposed backpacks could support everyday student routines.

**Decision 3: Simple Website Design**

Our research suggests that a clean layout with readable headings, product photographs, and organized information would fit our practical brand direction.

**Decision 4: Honest Persuasion**

CAMPUS LOOP should avoid fake reviews, unsupported product claims, invented discounts, and misleading information.

Any future product specifications must be verified before publication.

## 5. Customer Task and Testing Plan

**Selected Customer Task:** Help college students determine whether a backpack can carry their everyday school essentials.

**Research Finding:** Fjällräven clearly displays capacity, dimensions, and storage features.

**Proposed Feature:** A product-details section showing backpack dimensions, capacity, pocket organization, and verified laptop compatibility.

**Planned Test:** Ask a reviewer to open the original Fjällräven product page and identify the specifications supporting our proposed feature.

Once CAMPUS LOOP has a product page with verified information, ask students to locate those details without assistance.

**Testing Status:** Planned only. No customer usability testing has been completed.
